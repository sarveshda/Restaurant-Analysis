# Restaurant-Analysis
📊 EXPLANATION OF DATA (Restaurant Analytics Project)
The dataset used in this Power BI project contains information about restaurants and consumers across different cities and states.
 It helps analyze customer behavior, restaurant services, pricing, and overall performance.
🏪 Restaurant Data
This data provides details about each restaurant, including:
Restaurant Name
City & State
Cuisine Type
Price Range (Low, Medium, High)
Franchise Status (Yes / No)
Parking Availability (None, Public, Valet)
Alcohol Service (None, Wine & Beer, Full Bar)
Smoking Policy (No / Smoking Section / Yes)
📌 Purpose:
 To understand how restaurant facilities and services impact customer preference and ratings.
👥 Consumer Data
This data includes information about customers who visit restaurants:
Age Group (Young Adults, Adults, Seniors)
Occupation (Student, Employed, Unemployed)
Marital Status (Single, Married)
Drinking Level (Abstemious, Casual Drinker, Social Drinker)
Transportation Mode (Public Transport, Car, On Foot)
Budget Level (Low, Medium, High)
📌 Purpose:
 To analyze customer lifestyle, spending behavior, and dining habits.
⭐ Ratings & Reviews Data
This data captures customer feedback on restaurants:
Food Rating (Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory)
Service Rating
Overall Rating
Total Reviews Count
📌 Purpose:
 To identify top-performing restaurants and areas needing improvement.
🌍 Location Data
States: San Luis Potosi, Morelos, Tamaulipas
Cities: San Luis Potosi, Cuernavaca, Ciudad Victoria, Jiutepec
📌 Purpose:
 To compare restaurant performance and consumer behavior across regions.
🔍 Why This Data is Important
This dataset helps answer key business questions such as:
Which cuisines are most preferred?
Does price range affect customer satisfaction?
How do facilities like parking and alcohol service impact ratings?
Which cities and states have the highest restaurant demand?
What type of customers visit restaurants the most?
✅ Conclusion
The data enables a complete 360° analysis of the restaurant industry by combining restaurant features, consumer behavior, and review metrics.
 Using Power BI, this data is transformed into interactive dashboards that provide meaningful insights for business decision-making.
